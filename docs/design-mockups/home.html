Proposed National Initiative

Connecting Saudi Talent with Opportunity

A unified, intelligent national platform designed to seamlessly bridge the gap between job seekers and employers through advanced skill-based matching, comprehensive career guidance, and data-driven workforce insights.

The Challenge: A Fragmented Landscape

Currently, the employment ecosystem suffers from disconnected systems, manual processes, and information silos, leading to significant inefficiencies.

Fragmented Platforms

Job seekers must navigate dozens of disparate portals, while employers struggle to consolidate candidate pools.

Manual Hiring Processes

HR departments spend excessive time manually filtering resumes rather than engaging with qualified candidates.

Skill Mismatches

Educational outcomes often do not align with evolving industry requirements, creating a gap in the labor market.

The Solution: Intelligent Connection

A centralized platform utilizing smart algorithms to translate profiles and job descriptions into a common taxonomy of skills.

Core Platform Modules

Five integrated systems designed to deliver a comprehensive employment solution for the nation.

1. Smart Matching Engine

The core algorithm that evaluates non-obvious skill intersections to surface the best candidates for specific roles, reducing time-to-hire.

2. Employer Portal

A unified dashboard for organizations to post opportunities, manage talent pipelines, and access predictive hiring analytics in one place.

3. Career Guidance & Training

Integrated upskilling pathways and personalized career roadmaps aligned with current market demands and Vision 2030 objectives.

4. Data Analytics Dashboard

Macro-level insights for policymakers detailing real-time labor market trends, skill shortages, and employment demographics.

5. Professional Networking

Secure spaces for industry professionals, mentors, and fresh graduates to connect, fostering a collaborative national workforce.

Expected National Impact

Strategic outcomes aligning with national digital transformation goals.

Improved Labor Market Efficiency

Drastically reduced time-to-hire and lowered recruitment costs across both public and private sectors.

Reduced Skill Mismatches

Higher retention rates and job satisfaction by ensuring candidates are placed in roles fitting their true capabilities.

Empowered Workforce

Providing citizens with clear, actionable pathways for continuous learning and career advancement.

Informed Policymaking

Equipping government bodies with precise, real-time data to drive educational and economic strategies.
