1. Stakeholder Analysis

Stakeholder Classification Power Interest How they affect the project Communication needs Management strategy
MHRSD Sponsor / Internal High High Provides funding, defines ultimate strategic goals, approves major changes. Executive summaries, milestone reports, budget updates. Manage Closely
Job Seekers End User / External Low High Determines platform adoption rate. Requires intuitive UX and real value. Platform features, launch dates, support channels. Keep Informed
Employers End User / External Medium High Provides job listings. Their engagement is critical for platform success. Integration guides, feature updates, candidate quality metrics. Manage Closely
Students End User / External Low High Future workforce. Needs career guidance and skill mapping features. Skill requirements, internship opportunities, platform access. Keep Informed
Educational Institutions Partner / External Medium Medium Aligns curriculum with market needs based on platform data. Skills gap reports, integration APIs, partnership meetings. Keep Satisfied
Government Agencies Regulator / Internal High Low Ensures compliance with national digital standards and labor laws. Compliance audits, security reports, formal briefings. Keep Satisfied
Technology Partners Vendor / External High High Builds and maintains the infrastructure. Direct impact on delivery. Daily standups, technical specs, performance metrics. Manage Closely

4. Project Metrics

User Registration Rate

Rationale
Indicates initial platform adoption and marketing effectiveness among target demographics.
How it is measured
Total number of verified accounts created per week via the authentication service.
Frequency
Weekly
Responsible
Marketing & Analytics Team
Benefit to Project
Justifies ongoing funding and validates the demand for the platform in the market.

Successful Match Rate

Rationale
The core value proposition of the platform. Measures the effectiveness of the matching algorithm.
How it is measured
Percentage of job applications resulting in an interview or formal offer through the platform.
Frequency
Monthly
Responsible
Data Science Lead
Benefit to Project
Directly correlates with solving the national employment challenge; proves algorithmic efficacy.

System Uptime & Reliability

Rationale
Critical for maintaining user trust and ensuring continuous service availability nationwide.
How it is measured
Automated monitoring tools calculating the percentage of time core services are accessible.
Frequency
Real-time / Daily Report
Responsible
DevOps Manager
Benefit to Project
Ensures technical stability, minimizes reputational risk, and satisfies SLA requirements.

Employer Retention Rate

Rationale
Measures ongoing value for businesses. Without consistent employer usage, the platform fails.
How it is measured
Percentage of registered employers who post at least one new job within a 90-day period.
Frequency
Quarterly
Responsible
B2B Account Management
Benefit to Project
Indicates long-term sustainability and healthy supply side of the employment marketplace.
