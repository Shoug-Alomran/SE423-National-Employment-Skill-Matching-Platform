Four people, five deliverables, equal weight.

Work split for Scenario 11, the National Employment and Skill Matching Platform. Every member works on every artifact and carries 21 difficulty points in total, so nobody sits idle while someone else carries a whole deliverable.

Tap a name to rename it. Progress and names are saved in this browser.

Three rules that keep it fair

The course grades revisions and cohesion, so these matter as much as the tasks.

You own your sections

Whatever you write in one artifact, you revise in the next one after feedback. Every task below ends with that revision step.

Mark every change

Removed text is struck through and new or changed text is highlighted yellow, all inside one master document.

Sync before you split

Hold a 30-minute kickoff at the start of each artifact and a review call two days before the deadline. Hand-offs are named in the steps.

The sequence

Check the LMS for exact due dates. Each artifact includes every earlier one, improved.
